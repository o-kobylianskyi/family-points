## Definition of Done — coordinated product flow
1. Authorized family administrator opens a **reset preview** with category-specific counts, dependencies and exact scope; explicitly selects categories of disposable family data (TASK definitions/executions/history, REWARD definitions/requests/purchases, optional private templates, points/ledger/reputation, groups/media). State what stays intact: global account/auth, family identity/membership by default, secrets, globally bundled catalog, other families/workspaces; no implicit reset of unrelated family or external service.
2. Confirmation requires re-authentication/explicit phrase/password where feasible, server authorization and audit of actor, scope, timestamp, outcome. Backend performs integrity-safe ordered deletion/compensation per agreed policy, ensures no dangling references, no partial corrupt state, idempotent retry semantics. Test only on disposable fixture first. User expects eventual loss of synthetic test records but did not request deletion at this moment.
3. After successful reset, first-run/setup wizard offers *142 curated suggested templates and 6 packs*, selection of some/none, localized previews, customizable suggestions; imported selected items land ONLY in family-private template library; no automatic completed tasks, purchases, balances, ledger or unapproved purchases. Explicit separate activation of TASK/REWARD.
4. Standard currencies POINTS/COPPER/SILVER/GOLD with UK/RU/DE/EN word forms reconcile correctly after reset/onboarding without changing user-auth/account or foreign family data.
5. Preliminary economy v0.7 suggested difficulty independent of time, optional earned bonuses and reward costs, distinguish `null` vs zero, no automatic pricing of basic needs/family contact; parent reviews/edits recommendations. User calibration happens via BETA, never retroactive rewrite of balances.
6. Targeted QA verifies repeat reset/rebootstrap, partial selection, idempotent imports, same-family ledger integrity, cross-family isolation, permissions, boot/login. CI all jobs green and minimal deployed smoke before publication. Full visual/manual suite after early BETA.
